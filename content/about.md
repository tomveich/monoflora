Welcome to monoflora — a quiet corner of the internet for anyone who finds peace in petals, soil under their nails, and the rhythm of growth.

I’m Oliver. A person who believes that tending to plants is one of the most grounding, beautiful things you can do. Monoflora is my space to share the little joys of plant life — from creating natural bouquets to watching a seedling become a garden centerpiece.

Here, you’ll find stories, tips, inspirations, and reflections — all rooted in an honest love for flowers, foliage, and the art of care. Whether you're new to plants or have your own jungle at home, I hope you’ll feel at home here.
